Safety Tips

Your safety is our top priority. Follow these guidelines for a secure stay.

Verify Before You Go

Always check the host's profile and look for the "NID Verified" badge. Read reviews from other travellers to get a sense of the place and the host's hospitality.

Communicate via Platform

Keep all your communication and payments within the Traveller Stop platform. This allows us to protect your information and provides a record in case any issues arise.

Inspect the Property

Upon arrival, take a quick look around the property. Ensure all locks on doors and windows are functional. Locate emergency exits and fire extinguishers.

Keep Emergency Contacts Ready

Have local emergency numbers saved on your phone. In Bangladesh, dial 999 for immediate police, fire, or ambulance services.

Share Your Itinerary

Always let a friend or family member know where you are staying and provide them with the contact information of the host.

Need Help?

If you feel unsafe at any point during your stay, contact our 24/7 support line immediately at 01575467289.
